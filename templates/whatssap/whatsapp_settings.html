Security

RIBs and emails are encrypted with AES-256 (Fernet) before writing to the database. The server's secret key is required to decrypt them.

The UI shows only a masked version — last 4 characters for RIBs, full domain for emails.

How the AI uses this

For Digital Products, the AI lists your active methods, asks the customer to choose, shares that method's exact details, and requests a payment receipt screenshot — hands-free.

Bank vs. Digital Wallet

CIH / Attijariwafa / BOA — requires Account Holder Name + 24-digit RIB.
PayPal / Wise — requires only the account email.
